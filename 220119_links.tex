\section*{Architecture \& Workflow Summary}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Model:} 2-Layer CNN (PyTorch) -- Conv2d(3$\rightarrow$32) $\rightarrow$ ReLU $\rightarrow$ MaxPool2d(2) $\rightarrow$
        Conv2d(32$\rightarrow$64) $\rightarrow$ ReLU $\rightarrow$ MaxPool2d(2) $\rightarrow$ Flatten $\rightarrow$
        Linear(16384$\rightarrow$128) $\rightarrow$ ReLU $\rightarrow$ Linear(128$\rightarrow$3)
    \item \textbf{Input Preprocessing:} $64\times 64$ RGB images with single shared transform: $\text{Resize} \rightarrow \text{ToTensor} \rightarrow \text{Normalize}(\mu=0.5, \sigma=0.5)$
    \item \textbf{Training Setup:} CrossEntropyLoss, Adam optimizer ($\text{lr}=0.001$), batch size 64, 25 epochs
    \item \textbf{Dataset:} Geometric Shapes (Option 5) -- train 543, validation 180, test 90, plus 15 custom smartphone photos
    \item \textbf{Real-World Accuracy:} 12 / 15 (80.0\%) on custom smartphone photos (Circles: 4/5, Squares: 3/5, Triangles: 5/5) -- circle4.jpg correctly classified at 98.9\% confidence
    \item \textbf{Zero-Manual-Upload Automation:} Clones repo automatically in Google Colab, loads weights from \texttt{model/220119.pth}, evaluates test set, and predicts on custom photos automatically upon clicking ``Run All''.
\end{itemize}
